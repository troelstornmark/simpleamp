# tones/

Files here are bundled into **SimpleAmp Live** by `./scripts/build.sh`. They're not in git: Tone3000
captures are free to use but may not be redistributed.

```
tones/
├── <any pack folder>/   *.nam   amp captures (each folder = a group in the tone menu)
├── pedals/              *.nam   pedal captures for the NAM PEDAL slot
├── cabs/                *.wav   cabinet impulse responses
└── reverbs/             *.wav   reverb impulse responses
```

The tones used while building this:

- [Full Rig Peavey 5150 + Mesa 4x12](https://www.tone3000.com/tones/full-rig-peavey-5150-mesa-4x12-32868) by jpisoutoftune
- Peavey 6505 1992 Original - Rhythm Boosted - Mesa 4x12 - Full Rig (Tone3000, by outmodedelectronics)

You can also skip rebuilding: drop files into `~/Music/SimpleAmp/{amps,pedals,cabs,reverbs}` and they show up
in the app and the plugin within a few seconds.
